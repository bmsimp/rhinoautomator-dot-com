The framework

ARCHETYPES OF AUTOMATION

The biggest barrier to automation isn't technical skill. It's process thinking, team composition, and role awareness. The framework maps 8 dimensions of automation capability so teams can find their gaps and fill them.

INDIVIDUAL SELF-ASSESSMENT

Score yourself across 8 archetypes to see where you contribute most and where you have room to grow.

GAP ANALYSIS
